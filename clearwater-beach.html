Dog Walking & Pet Sitting in Clearwater Beach

Professional, licensed, and insured pet care serving Clearwater Beach and nearby communities in Pinellas County — your local pet sitter near me for the barrier islands. GPS-tracked walks, photo updates, and reliable scheduling — whether you're a year-round resident or visiting for the season.

Clearwater Beach is one of Florida's most visited beach destinations — and it's packed with seasonal residents, vacation rental guests, and snowbirds who need reliable pet care while they enjoy the sand and sun. Whether you're staying at a beachfront condo for the week or living on the island year-round, Spare Human Services provides licensed, insured dog walking and pet sitting just 15 minutes away in Largo.

Visitors staying at resorts near Pier 60 or vacation rentals along Gulf Boulevard often need someone to walk their dog while they spend the day on the beach (Clearwater Beach itself is not dog-friendly on the sand), grab dinner at Frenchy's, or take a sunset sail. That's where I come in — GPS-tracked walks, photo updates after every visit, and scheduling that works around your vacation plans, not the other way around.

For full-time residents on the beach or in the nearby Island Estates neighborhood, I offer regular weekly walking schedules, drop-in visits for pets who need midday potty breaks, and overnight care when you travel. Same licensed, insured care whether you're here for a weekend or here for life.
